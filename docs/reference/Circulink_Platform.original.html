How a trade works

Four steps from collection to compliance record

STEP 1

List

A collector or household posts material, weight and location.

STEP 2

Match

Verified buyers within range see the listing and make an offer.

STEP 3

Weigh and pay

Weight is confirmed at pickup. Payment is released through escrow.

STEP 4

Record

The batch lands in the buyer's EPR audit trail automatically.

            Design system

            How Circulink looks and behaves

            The tokens and components behind every screen. Green builds structure, charcoal carries text, orange marks the one thing that needs attention.

            Colour

            Switch the theme to see the dark values
            Distribution

            About 70% alabaster and tints, 20% charcoal, 8% green, no more than 2% orange.

            Orange rule

            One orange element per screen: the number, warning or step the eye must land on.

            Status colour

            Red is for errors only. Blue marks information. Neither replaces the brand colours.

            Layout rules

            Grid

            Twelve columns with 24px gutters. Pages use a 60/40 split, a three-card row or a four-step flow.

            Radius

            24px on cards, 16px on inner blocks, 10px on inputs, full round on buttons and pills.

            Numbers lead

            Key figures are set large in Montserrat, with a short label and one line of context.

            Process rows

            Steps sit in equal containers joined by a green line with an arrowhead. The step that matters is outlined in orange.

            Words

            Three short points at most. Buttons say exactly what happens. Errors say how to fix it.

            Spacing

            Cards pad 24px, sections sit 24px apart, page margins are 32px and drop to 16px on phones.
